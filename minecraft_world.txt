ChatGPT Minecraft World

Turning AI waiting time into a miniature, animated world.

ChatGPT Minecraft World is an experimental Chrome extension that transforms the ChatGPT experience into a Minecraft-inspired construction scene. While ChatGPT generates a response, a miniature world comes to life with houses, castles, skyscrapers, workers, trees, and animated building effects.

Sometimes, the world grows. Sometimes, it goes BOOM!


FEATURES

- Procedural Buildings: Watch houses and other structures appear across a growing landscape.
- Medieval Castles: Explore a fantasy-inspired architectural world.
- Fairy Castles: Discover magical castles surrounded by sparkling effects.
- City Skyline: Mix residential buildings with skyscrapers and urban structures.
- Animated Workers: Watch construction workers bring the miniature world to life.
- Living Landscape: Trees and environmental elements add variety to the scene.
- Explosion Effect: Trigger a dramatic explosion animation with a keyboard press.
- Manual Demo Mode: Start the animation manually to demonstrate the extension.
- Browser Integration: Designed to run as a Chrome extension alongside ChatGPT.


THE IDEA :

AI interfaces are usually built around text boxes, loading indicators, and streaming responses.

This project explores a different question:

What if waiting for an AI response felt like watching a world being built?

The idea is to make the waiting experience more playful by creating an animated miniature world alongside the ChatGPT interface.

Rather than another conventional loading screen, this project experiments with creative visualisation, interactive animation, and unexpected browser experiences.

PROJECT STATUS :

This is an experimental creative-coding project.

Features and behaviour may change as development continues. The animation is a visual companion to the ChatGPT interface and does not represent ChatGPT's internal reasoning or computational activity.

The project explores how creative coding and animation can make everyday digital interactions more engaging.
